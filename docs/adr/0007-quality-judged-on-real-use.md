# 0007. Quality is judged on real use, by the owner, not a paid judge

Date: 15 Oct 2026 (verdicts built 23 Oct) · Status: Accepted · Decided by: the owner

## Context
The plan proposed a paid baseline: freeze a set of tasks and have a model judge old and new answers
blind. The owner declined: the old answers were not worth paying to measure. Adding more judge
models beside the current one was also put on hold (16 Oct: licence and hosting).

## Decision
Quality is the owner's verdict on real answers (👍/👎 under each reply and each finished task,
"Redo with full team"), citations checked in code, and what each request costs in calls, time and
money, lead beside team (Settings → Performance). No paid judge runs.

## Consequences
The measure grows only as the owner presses the buttons. A finding is read from real use after a
week or two, not from a benchmark.
